What it does, plainly

Live memory and storage

Two rings show how full your RAM and storage are. They turn amber at 65% and red at 85%, so a glance tells you if something is wrong.

Battery at a glance

Charge level, charging state, health, voltage and battery temperature, in Celsius or Fahrenheit.

Duplicate photo finder

Finds photos that are exactly the same file and tells you how much space the extra copies take. It only reports; you decide what to do.

Unused app list

Lists apps you have not opened in 30 days. Tap one to open its settings. HyperClean never uninstalls anything for you.

Floating monitor

Optional small overlay that shows live RAM over other apps. Turn it off any time.

Cache cleaner

Clears HyperClean's own cache after you confirm. It cannot touch your files or other apps' data.

Honest by design

Questions

Is it safe?

The app has no internet access and sends nothing anywhere. Download it only from this page, and check that the version number matches.

Why does it ask for photo access?

Only for the duplicate photo scan, and only when you tap scan. You can choose "selected photos" on newer Android, or skip the scan entirely.

Why does it ask for usage access?

Android only tells apps when you last opened other apps if you grant this special permission. It is used for the unused app list and nothing else.

Will it make my phone faster?

Not by itself. It helps you see what is filling your storage and which apps you no longer use, which is where real space and speed gains come from.

Is it on Google Play?

Not yet. For now you install it directly from this page.

Privacy policy

HyperClean does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data. It does not use the internet.

  • Memory, storage, battery: read from your device to show on screen.
  • Photos: read only when you tap scan, to compare files for exact duplicates. Photos are not copied, changed, deleted or uploaded.
  • App usage: read only with the Usage access permission you grant, to list apps not opened for 30 days.
  • Settings and a lifetime "cleaned" counter: kept only on your phone and removed when you uninstall.
